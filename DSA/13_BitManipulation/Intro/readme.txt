| Operator    | Symbol | Example (a=5, b=3) | Result | Explanation          |   
| ----------- | ------ | ------------------ | ------ | -------------------- | 
| AND         | `&`    | `5 & 3`            | `1`    | `0101 & 0011 = 0001` |    
| OR          | \`     | \`                 | \`5    | 3\`                  | 
| XOR         | `^`    | `5 ^ 3`            | `6`    | `0101 ^ 0011 = 0110` |     
| NOT         | `~`    | `~5`               | `-6`   | Bitwise negation     |   
| Left Shift  | `<<`   | `5 << 1`           | `10`   | `0101 << 1 = 1010`   |     
| Right Shift | `>>`   | `5 >> 1`           | `2`    | `0101 >> 1 = 0010`   |
